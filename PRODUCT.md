# Product

GPA Planner: a local-only web app and Chrome extension where a university student types grades and sees the score needed on every remaining item to reach a goal grade and CGPA target.

## Users
University students (first target: AITU, Kazakhstan, Moodle). Used in short sessions on laptop and phone, often stressed before exams.

## Tone
Calm, familiar, quiet. Reference feel: Telegram and Instagram. No hype, no gamification.

## Principles
- The needed score is the hero; everything else supports it.
- Local first: nothing leaves the device except requests to the student's own Moodle.
- Keyboard fast: type grades, Enter to move on, Ctrl K to jump.
- Few borders, few weights, system font.

## Anti-references
Dense dashboards, neon dark mode, numbered section labels, monospace labels, heavy shadows.
